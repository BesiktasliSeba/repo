MacStatusBar&Dock

A macOS-style desktop for your iPad

A menu bar with real app menus, Mac windows, a magnifying Dock and much more. It works with just your fingers or with a keyboard and trackpad or mouse, in every orientation. Optional extras, like the auto-hiding menu bar, start switched off, and most features have their own switch in Settings.

Features

Menu bar

A real Mac menu bar: an Apple menu (About This iPad, App Store, Force Quit, Respring, Safe Mode, Lock Screen, Sleep, Shut Down) and menus for the app in front (App, Edit, Go, Window), in every orientation, mirrored in right-to-left languages as on a Mac. Tap the clock for a Today drop-down with your widgets and notifications. Audio mixing per app, with a volume for each app. A VPN menu with Disconnect, and a Wi-Fi menu to switch networks without Control Center. Status Bar Style switches between Mac and the stock iPadOS bar.

Windows

Mac windows on the engine you already use (Aerial 3.0, 5.0 or 5.1, Zetsu 1.6.2 or 1.6.6, MilkyWay4 on iPadOS 15): title bars, traffic lights, rounded corners, resize handles and Fit to Window tiling. Or Stage Manager as the window engine (iPadOS 16 to 18): Apple's own windowing with the Mac look, over your Home Screen and desktop. Windows stay where you put them and at the size you give them, also when you go Home, and an app can have more than one window. Only the window you're using keeps a text cursor, and Esc ends typing. Windows have a smallest size with every engine, as in Stage Manager. With Aerial, Zetsu or MilkyWay4 on iPadOS 15 to 18, every app with a window also gets its App Switcher card.

Mac Switcher

Desktops and Mission Control like on a Mac, in place of the App Switcher (Settings > Windows & Multitasking > Mac Switcher, off until you switch it on). Swipe up and hold, double press the Home button or press Control-Up on a keyboard to see your desktops and windows. Tap + to add a desktop (up to 8), swipe sideways with four fingers or along the bottom edge and the desktops slide with your fingers, and drag a window onto another desktop to move it. Desktops work with every window engine.

Finder

A Mac Finder window on every engine: sidebar, list and icon views, Quick Look, Search, Undo and Put Back, several-item selection and drag and drop into apps. On My iPad shows the same folders as the Files app, and the Trash has an Empty button. A desktop on the first Home Screen page for your files and folders, with their menus over your windows and names you can hide, and a small TextEdit window for text files. It only changes files in your own places, so it can't be used to break the iPad by accident.

Drives in Finder

USB sticks, SSDs and SD card readers show up under Locations as soon as you connect them. exFAT and FAT32 work (FAT32 holds no files over 4 GB); APFS and Mac OS Extended should work too but are not tested yet; NTFS is not supported by iPadOS. SSDs may need more power: connect them straight to the iPad's USB-C port or use a powered hub.

Dock

A macOS Dock: magnification, recent apps, running-app dots, Finder and Launchpad at its start like on a Mac, and a Downloads stack with search that you can drag files into and out of. A Trash at its end, off until you turn it on in Settings > Dock: drop files on it to move them to the Trash, a Dock app to take it out of the Dock, or a drive to eject it on iPadOS 16 and 17 (on iPadOS 18, ejecting doesn't work yet).

Activity Monitor

A Mac-style Activity Monitor window with the running processes and their CPU, memory and disk use: sort, search, inspect a process and quit apps. Open it from the Go menu, the RAM & CPU panel in Control Center or Spotlight's actions. System processes show no figures, and the Energy and Network tabs have none per process. It's also an app in the App Library, in Utilities and in its search.

Built to be safe

Automatic Crash Recovery: if a feature is followed by two SpringBoard crashes in a row, it switches itself off instead of leaving you in a crash loop, tells you in Settings and offers Report a Problem; another tweak's crash never switches MacStatusBar&Dock off. Off by default on untested iPadOS versions (unless you choose Enable Anyway), does nothing on an iPhone, and never contacts any server.

Also included

A Mac pointer (arrow and I-beam, with Pointer Control colors as an option), Mac-style notification banners, Mac-style Haptic Touch menus (Force Quit, App Size and Add to Folder on app icons, folder shortcuts), a mute icon, Control Center from the menu bar, a RAM & CPU control for Control Center, a customizable Go menu, an auto-hiding menu bar, Home Screen options (page dots, icon labels, Home Bar), Lock Screen options, keyboard extras (Cmd-Tab to the right window, Esc closes menus, Tab to mute, Globe volume and brightness keys), longer Auto-Lock times, a search field in the Settings sidebar, Spotlight extras you can turn on (tweak settings, actions, your windows, your Desktop and Downloads files), an SSH switch, Ethernet settings and Reduce Motion support.

The Mac look is on right after install, and most features have their own switch in Settings > Status Bar, Windows & Multitasking and Dock. Some extras start off, such as the Mac Switcher, the Dock's Trash, the auto-hiding menu bar, seconds in the clock, the keyboard extras and the Lock Screen options. Apps open full screen until you tap the green light or turn on Window > Open Apps as Windows (with the Stage Manager engine they open as windows from the start).

Which window engine?

On iPadOS 16.1 to 18, an iPad with Stage Manager (or an older iPad with TrollPad) starts with Stage Manager, Apple's own windowing with the Mac look, tested on iPadOS 16.7.7, 17.5.1 and 18.5. Anywhere else, start with Aerial. Zetsu works well too and is a good alternative. MilkyWay4 runs on iPadOS 15 only. Updating keeps the engine you picked.

Report a Problem

The Report a Problem button (Settings > Status Bar, and the Automatic Crash Recovery note) opens a new GitHub issue in your browser, filled in with your iPad model, iPadOS version, window engine and its version, and the tweak's version. If the crash protection switched something off in the last 7 days, it adds what it turned off and when, and a few lines naming the tweak's own code that crashed (nothing from other apps or tweaks); on an untested iPadOS version, which of the tweak's switches are on. The report is part of the link to GitHub, so GitHub receives its text when the page opens. You see the whole text there, can change or delete any of it, and it's only posted as an issue if you submit it (a GitHub account is needed). Nothing personal is included, and the tweak collects nothing in the background.

Known limitations

  • The tweak's own menus and Settings pages are in English only.
  • Apple's Stage Manager (iPadOS 16 to 18) stays off while another window engine runs, because two window systems would fight over the same apps. To use it, pick Stage Manager as the window engine in Settings > Windows & Multitasking > Window Engine.
  • With the Stage Manager engine, a desktop holds up to 7 windows.
  • Some apps, like many games, only run full screen. The camera works only full screen (iPadOS pauses it in a window), and Photos opened from the Camera stays full screen.
  • Finder can't show iCloud Drive (iPadOS doesn't allow it), drag and drop goes from Finder into apps but not back yet, and drives have been tested on iPadOS 15 only.
  • On iPadOS 15, Safari's sound can stop another app that plays alone, as on a stock iPad. Control Center's Now Playing shows one app at a time.
  • While Destra is on, it shows the notification banners instead.
  • Aerial 5.0 and 5.1 have to be activated in their own settings before they open windows.
  • With Aerial 5.0 or 5.1, don't respring while a VPN is on or was just turned off: Aerial goes online as SpringBoard starts and can hang on a black screen until you restart the iPad. MacStatusBar&Dock warns before resprings that go through iOS.
  • The keys that work inside apps (Shift + = / - for the screen brightness, Tab as Mute) do nothing while a package manager, Filza, iCleaner, TrollStore or the jailbreak app is in front: the tweak leaves those apps alone, so they always open even if something goes wrong.

Both parts switched off? If you turn off both MacStatusBar and MacDock in Settings, their Settings pages go away too. Turn them back on in Choicy (or iCleaner Pro), then respring.

Requirements

  • A hooking platform: ElleKit, libhooker, or Substrate
  • Choicy or iCleaner Pro (keeps only one window engine loaded)
  • Optional window engine: Aerial 3.0, 5.0 or 5.1, MilkyWay4 0.1.1 (iPadOS 15 only), Zetsu 1.6.2 or 1.6.6. Other versions run as plain windows, without the Mac look.

External display (iPadOS 16, Stage Manager engine)

Works:

  • Its own desktop: menu bar (Apple menu, app menus, status items), Dock, wallpaper.
  • Windows with title bars and traffic lights; the front window on the display drives its menu bar.
